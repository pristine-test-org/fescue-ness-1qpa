How Bittern works

One shared list, one calm page: Bittern shows the five newest items so you can see what is moving without opening anything else.

  1. Someone adds an item to the shared list.
  2. Bittern reads the five newest items when the page loads.
  3. Finished items carry a check mark, open ones stay plain.

Every visit reads straight from the shared list, so what you see here matches what the rest of the team sees at that moment, with nothing cached in between.
